# /brag Plan: Second Innings (Vertical / Portrait Edition)

## Planning Rubric (Vertical Launch)

1. **What is the product?**
   Second Innings — a dedicated mentoring platform for youth aged 16–25 and their parents, based in Jaipur, Rajasthan and serving students pan-India.

2. **Who is it for?**
   Students facing career transitions, stream choices, university selections, or feeling lost amid information overload, plus parents seeking guidance without control.

3. **What is the single sharpest problem it solves?**
   Information is abundant everywhere, but unbiased, experienced, human-led perspective and actionable clarity are rare.

4. **What is the founder's background?**
   Deepak Sogani: 35+ years across corporate leadership, entrepreneurship, and 5 years as Head of Student Affairs at JK Lakshmipat University (JKLU), mentoring thousands of students.

5. **What is the core methodology?**
   A structured 7-step approach: `LISTEN` → `UNDERSTAND` → `EXPLORE` → `PERSPECTIVE` → `CONNECT` → `ACT` → `REVIEW`, leading to a concrete `7-Day Next Step`.

6. **What is the format & aspect ratio?**
   Vertical / Portrait (9:16 aspect ratio, 1080×1920 at 30fps). Optimized for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, WhatsApp Status, TikTok, and mobile-first video players.

7. **What is the duration?**
   Exactly 20.0 seconds (600 frames at 30fps).

8. **What is the audio landscape?**
   Warm, inspiring acoustic/indie rhythm (`bgm.mp3`), coupled with subtle analog interface clicks/switches on key visual reveals.

9. **What is the call to action?**
   "Your first introductory conversation is complimentary. No pressure. No selling." → `Start a Conversation` at `second-innings.in`.
