2. ABOUT SECOND INNINGS

A Space for Perspective, Possibilities and Action

Second Innings is an initiative created to support young people as they navigate the choices, transitions and possibilities that shape their lives beyond the classroom.

It is built on the belief that preparing for life requires more than academic achievement or access to information. Young people also benefit from opportunities to understand themselves, broaden their exposure, consider different perspectives and develop the confidence to make choices they can own.

Second Innings brings these elements together through individual conversations, meaningful exposure, exploration of opportunities and thoughtful follow-through.

It is deliberately different from a conventional counselling, coaching or motivational model. There are no ready-made prescriptions and no attempt to define success for the young person.

Instead, the approach is to listen before responding, understand before suggesting, explore before narrowing choices, and encourage action rather than dependence.

The purpose is simple:

To help young people see more, think more clearly and move forward with greater ownership of their choices.

Why “Second Innings”?

After more than three decades across the corporate world, entrepreneurship and higher education, Deepak Sogani chose to dedicate the next phase of his professional life to working with young people.

His experience of working closely with university students reinforced something he had come to value deeply: some of the most meaningful contributions happen through conversations that help a young person gain perspective, discover an opportunity, reconsider a choice or take a meaningful next step.

That experience gave direction to what came next.

The first innings was about building his own journey.The Second Innings is about using that experience to contribute to the journeys of young people.

And from that idea, Second Innings was born.

5. HOW IT WORKS

One Conversation Can Be a Beginning

Second Innings does not begin with a presentation, a questionnaire full of scores or a predetermined solution.

It begins with you.

01 — TALK

What's on your mind?You don't need to arrive with a perfectly framed question. Sometimes even “I am confused” is enough to begin.

02 — UNDERSTAND

We look beyond the immediate question.What matters to you? What are you experiencing? What are your concerns? What might be influencing your thinking?Understanding comes before advice.

03 — EXPLORE

There may be possibilities you haven't considered yet.Together, we explore different perspectives, alternatives, opportunities and questions worth thinking about.

04 — CHOOSE YOUR NEXT STEP

The objective isn't for someone else to make the decision for you.It is to help you move towards a next step that makes sense to you and that you are willing to own.

05 — FOLLOW THROUGH

Where appropriate, we reconnect.What did you try? What happened? What did you discover? What should happen next?Because a meaningful conversation becomes more valuable when it leads to action.

8. FOR PARENTS

Every parent wants their child to make thoughtful choices and build a meaningful future.

But as young people grow, they also need opportunities to question, explore and gradually take ownership of their decisions.

Second Innings seeks to complement—not replace—the role of parents, teachers, educational institutions or qualified professionals.

The objective is not to decide a young person's future for them.

It is to provide an additional space for thoughtful conversation, broader perspective and exploration—helping young people become more confident in making choices they understand and own.

Where appropriate, parents may also become part of the broader conversation while respecting the young person's privacy and independence.

10. CONFIDENTIALITY & PROFESSIONAL BOUNDARIES

Short Version for the Website/Form

Your Conversation Matters

Second Innings aims to provide a respectful and non-judgmental space where young people can speak openly.

Personal information and conversations are treated with discretion and respect for privacy, subject to appropriate safety, safeguarding and legal responsibilities.

[ READ PRIVACY & PROFESSIONAL BOUNDARIES ]

Full Version: Privacy, Safety & Professional Boundaries

Second Innings is committed to providing a respectful, responsible and non-judgmental environment for young people.

Information shared during conversations is treated with discretion and respect for privacy.

However, confidentiality cannot be absolute.

Where there is a reasonable concern involving risk of serious harm, personal safety, abuse, harassment or another situation requiring responsible intervention, appropriate steps may need to be taken. Depending on the circumstances, this could include encouraging or involving a parent/guardian, educational institution, qualified professional or other appropriate support.

Second Innings is not a medical, psychological, psychiatric, legal or emergency service.

Conversations through Second Innings should not be considered a substitute for qualified mental-health counselling, therapy, medical care or other specialist professional services.

Where a matter falls outside the scope of Second Innings, the young person may be encouraged to seek appropriate professional support.

For participants below the age of 18, appropriate parental/guardian consent and safeguarding requirements will apply.

The purpose of these boundaries is not to restrict conversation, but to ensure that Second Innings remains a safe, responsible and professionally appropriate space.
